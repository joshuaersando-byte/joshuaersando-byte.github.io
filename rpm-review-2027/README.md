# RPM 2027 Review Hub

Responsive, installable six-month review app for the Philippine Psychometrician Licensure Examination (RPM/PmLE).

## Live site
https://joshuaersando-byte.github.io/rpm-review-2027/

## Curriculum
The study architecture follows the 2026 Psychometrician board subject structure and the PRC enhanced Table of Specifications:

- Developmental Psychology — 20%
- Abnormal Psychology — 20%
- Industrial-Organizational Psychology — 20%
- Psychological Assessment / Psychometrics — 40%

## Included in this build
- 26-week review plan
- 80 original practice questions with rationales
- 42 active-recall flashcards
- 24 high-yield review-note modules
- weighted mixed practice and mock-board mode
- error notebook and weak-area tracking
- subject mastery tracking
- study streak and quiz history
- bookmarks
- local progress persistence
- progress export/import
- responsive phone, tablet, iPad and desktop layouts
- installable PWA/offline support
